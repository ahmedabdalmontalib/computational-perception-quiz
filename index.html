How this quiz works

  1. One question at a time, and nothing to type: click, tap, drag a slider or choose from a list.
  2. Press Check. Your answer is locked, the explanation appears, and it tells you where to read again in the lab sheet.
  3. Move with Next and Previous, or jump with the question map: each square is one question, green when right and red when wrong.
  4. At the end, press Finish for your score card. Then Redo my mistakes gives you only the questions you got wrong, in a new order.
